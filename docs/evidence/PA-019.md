## Implemented boundary

- Initiative is opt-in and starts off. A rule pins an active goal revision, anchor task, exact verified source account and thread, and the configured model disclosure route. Stored event replay is deterministic and uses up to 100 scoped events. Admission checks source freshness, keywords, content dedupe, cooldown, price and budget before semantic assessment.
- Observe records proposals and suppression reasons. Prepare requires at least 20 owner-reviewed proposals with at least 70% useful and a specific source-read grant. It creates a private, source-linked artifact. Act additionally requires a useful prepared brief and an explicit grant to surface private briefs in Today.
- Local notices use the same three-per-owner-per-local-day budget as existing external reservations. Quiet hours apply before publication. A model response with no useful proposal remains silent. No proactive path creates tasks, sends external messages or arms a schedule.
- Rule, source, goal, task and policy fences apply after assessment and before artifact or notice publication. Versioned decisions and evidence survive restart. Interrupted assessments are suppressed rather than reissued. Schema 011 preserves existing work and recovery mode and creates a private v10 backup.
