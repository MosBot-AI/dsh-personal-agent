## Implemented boundary

- SQLite atomically reserves and claims a configured ceiling in integer currency millionths before model generation. Admission checks exact provider and model, current Task and Attempt, owner UTC day, Task totals, calls and concurrency. Automatic requests require known or explicitly unmetered pricing. Owner-started unknown-price work uses bounded call and resource limits, with its cost shown separately.
- Usage reconciliation records disjoint input, output, cache-read and cache-write counts at the price stored with the claim. Cold open releases never-claimed reservations and marks interrupted claims uncertain. Their ceilings continue to count until exact reconciliation, including across UTC day boundaries. Schema 006 creates a private v5 backup and preserves routing, Worker input and restore state.
- Native Worker generation and Router assessment share the meter. Workers have no model tools. Input, output, turns, steps and wall time have validated ceilings. The native Worker prevents automatic provider retry after 429. Worker and Router requests share exact-route cooldown in process, and a Worker wait records a durable wake time or indefinite hold. The owner can requeue a released eligible wait after the cooldown. Foreground controls keep their separate path.
- The in-process GPU queue has one default heavy slot and bounded FIFO waiting. The Task and Attempt are checked at handoff. Cancellation or deadline signals the operation and keeps its physical slot until teardown settles.
- Versioned attention policy coalesces routine progress into the task view and records suppression or reservation reasons. Proactive `off`, `observe`, `prepare` and `act` use distinct decisions. Quiet hours use the selected IANA zone. An owner can reserve up to three unsolicited notices per local day across destinations. Explicit reminders and required decisions are counted separately. A single-use attention handoff rechecks exact destination, task, policy and scope, then invokes the same store's Outbox. PA-007 still owns routing, disclosure, FIFO and provider receipts.
- The authenticated inbox exposes compute and attention usage, uncertainty, queue occupancy, progress and recent decision reasons. A missing price for the optional assessor leaves the foreground inbox available.
