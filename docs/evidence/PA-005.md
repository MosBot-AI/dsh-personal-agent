## Implemented boundary

- Trusted adapters bind exact owner, profile, device, client, and session identities, or the verified account of a specific source instance. Opaque identity handles are checked against current enrollment and policy and can be released by the adapter. Source handles provide intake identity. Approval responses require the exact owner session and enrollment.
- The action gate checks a registered connector's exact account, resource, recipient, payload validator, and disclosure scope against the current Attempt. An eligible prepared action produces a review request with an immutable preview and a SHA-256 digest of the bounded canonical action. Approval binds its digest, policy, task, attempt, generation, enrollment, and expiry. Observed expiry persists across restart and clock rewind.
- The SQLite transaction rechecks the exact proposal, approval, account and device enrollment, current task version and attempt generation, revocation, and expiry. It reserves the single-use grant and advances the effect to `dispatching` atomically. The trusted connector starts the exact stored action synchronously after commit. A failed claim invokes no handoff. An interrupted or uncertain handoff leaves the consumed claim available for reconciliation.
- `ctx.dshpa.host.privateWorkers` installs a scoped tool-free executor and deny-only guard before native publication on create and resume. Private Workers publish zero tool definitions. Scripted shell, browser, network, file, MCP, late scoped, and reserved PTC calls were denied. Ordinary Agent behavior remained available. Fixture credential markers stayed outside model messages and persisted sessions.
- Schema 002 preserves version 1 work and adds proposal, approval, grant, and dispatch records with exact foreign keys. A guarded upgrade takes an owner-private `state.sqlite.v1.bak` snapshot including committed WAL data before additive DDL. Capacity exhaustion retains the version 1 database and its backup for retry.

The dispatch capability belongs to trusted coordinator code. Connector validators check payload shape, ownership, and disclosure, and credential access stays in trusted adapters. The private Worker caller verifies persisted session ownership and the model route on resume. Trusted DSH composition callbacks remain within the host trust boundary.
