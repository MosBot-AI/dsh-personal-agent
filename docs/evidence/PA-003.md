## Implemented boundary

- Strict decoders define the durable records, distinguish branded identities and require canonical UTC instants. Task, Attempt, Conversation, Effect, and Delivery remain separate. Effect and Delivery retain uncertainty independently of provider receipts.
- Owner commands take authenticated identity from the caller and enforce owner, profile, and any supplied trusted project and conversation scope. Allocations and mandate references come from the caller. Worker reports are restricted to readiness and waiting, with the dispatch revision bound to the authenticated attempt.
- The reducer checks versions and generations and computes state without I/O. It supports exact waits, validates a dependency chain supplied by the scheduler, and distinguishes verification from worker readiness. Cancellation advances the task generation before any external stop. A trusted coordinator settles only the exact retired attempt. Retry and reopen allocate a new attempt and return the previous one for durable history.
- The durable Task and Attempt decoder rejects mismatched identities, phases, generations, unstarted attempts with runtime bindings, and completed records missing required artifact or receipt references.
